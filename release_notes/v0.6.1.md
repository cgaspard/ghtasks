### Fixes

- **Clicking the menu-bar icon opens the app again on macOS 27.** On macOS 27, a left click on the icon showed the Show / Hide / Quit menu instead of opening the task window. A left click now opens and closes the window as it should, and a right click still shows the menu.

### Notes

- Under the hood, this release moves to Tauri 2.12. It brings in the upstream tray fix for macOS 27's new click handling in the menu bar.
- Still on an older version and stuck behind the menu? Choose **Show** from it to open the window.

### Install

Download `GitHub.Tasks_universal.dmg` (signed + notarized), open it, and drag GitHub Tasks to Applications. Existing installs update themselves, and your sources, token, and settings carry over.
