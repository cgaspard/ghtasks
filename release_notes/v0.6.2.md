### Changed

- **The Issues tab is now called Todos.** Same list, new name: open issues from every repository you track, filtered by your own search. "Todos" says what the tab is for, a list of things to get done, and it reads more clearly next to Projects and Inbox.

### Notes

- Nothing to do on your side. Your repositories, filters, and last-opened tab carry over unchanged.

### Install

Download `GitHub.Tasks_universal.dmg` (signed + notarized), open it, and drag GitHub Tasks to Applications. Existing installs update themselves, and your sources, token, and settings carry over.
